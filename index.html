About

Animal presets are rough sketches from published descriptions, not measured fits. Ultrasonic animals (bats, porpoise, moths) are pitched down into the audible range, as on a time-expansion bat detector. Character blends between three click types loosely based on the paper's three echolocators. Saved presets live in this browser only; use "Move presets between devices" to carry them over. On iPhone, switch off silent mode. Space bar fires one group.

Synthetic echolocation clicks after Thaler et al. 2017, extended with ideas from Peter Blasser's click synth. Each slider moves several parameters at once.
